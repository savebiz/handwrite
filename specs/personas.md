# Specs: Target Personas

1. **Scanning Operator**: Scans paper forms, needs instant feedback on image blur, skew, or cut-offs (`rescan_required`).
2. **Quality Control Reviewer**: Reviews flagged handwriting crops, approves/corrects proposed values, ensures 100% field accuracy.
3. **Records & Governance Officer**: Configures sensitivity policies, audit rules, and compliance data retention.
4. **Onboarding Operations Lead**: Monitors throughput, error rates, and reviewer processing time per record family.
